Returns & Refunds

Find out how to request help with an incorrect, damaged or problematic order.

Return and Exchange Requests

If an item arrives damaged, incorrect or has another issue, contact Nishat Fashion and provide your order number plus a clear description of the problem. Photos may help the team assess the request.

Eligibility

Return or exchange eligibility depends on the store's confirmed policy and the condition of the item. Please keep the product, packaging and order information until your request has been reviewed.

How to Request Help

  1. Open the Contact Us page.
  2. Include your order number and the item concerned.
  3. Describe the issue and provide supporting photos if requested.
  4. Wait for instructions from customer support before sending an item back.

Refunds

If a refund is approved, the store should explain the available refund method and expected processing steps. Timing may depend on the payment provider and financial institution.

Items That Cannot Be Accepted

Any exclusions, hygiene-related restrictions, time limits or condition requirements must be confirmed in the store's final published policy. Please contact support before returning an item.
