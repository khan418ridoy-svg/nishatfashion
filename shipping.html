Shipping & Delivery

Review important information about delivery and receiving your order.

Delivery Coverage

Delivery availability depends on the destination address and the options shown during checkout. Please enter your address and contact number accurately so the order can be processed.

Delivery Charges

The applicable delivery charge should be shown during checkout before you confirm your order. Charges may vary by delivery area. If a charge is unclear, contact customer support before placing the order.

Processing and Delivery Time

Order processing and delivery times can vary depending on product availability, destination and courier operations. Any estimated delivery information provided by the store should be treated as an estimate rather than a guaranteed arrival time.

Address and Contact Details

  • Provide a complete address, including area, district and useful landmarks where applicable.
  • Provide a reachable phone number for delivery coordination.
  • Contact support promptly if your address or phone number needs to be corrected.

Delivery Issues

If a parcel is delayed, the address is incorrect or delivery cannot be completed, contact Nishat Fashion with your order details so the team can review the available next steps.
