1. Shipping & Delivery

Nishat Fashion aims to process and deliver orders using the delivery details provided by customers.

Delivery Address

Customers should provide a complete address, district, area or thana, and a reachable phone number at checkout. Incorrect or incomplete information may delay delivery.

Delivery Charges

Applicable delivery charges will be shown during checkout before the order is placed. The amount shown in the final order summary is the amount applicable to that order.

Delivery Time

Delivery times may vary depending on the destination, courier availability, weather and other circumstances. Any delivery estimate shown during checkout or communicated by the store should be used as the applicable estimate.

If you need to correct a delivery address, contact customer support as soon as possible. A change may not be possible after an order has been dispatched.

2. Returns & Exchanges

If a product arrives damaged, incorrect or has another issue, contact Nishat Fashion and provide your order number and a clear explanation of the issue.

Requesting Help

  • Keep your order details available.
  • Describe the issue with the product.
  • Provide supporting photos when requested.
  • Wait for instructions before sending a product back.

Eligibility and Resolution

Return, exchange, refund and cancellation requests must be reviewed according to the applicable store policy and relevant consumer protection requirements. Approval, timelines and available remedies depend on the circumstances of the order.

Do not assume that a return, exchange or refund has been approved until you receive confirmation from customer support.

3. Payment Policy

The payment method available for an order is the method displayed at checkout. Nishat Fashion currently presents bKash as its checkout payment option.

Payment Confirmation

Customers should follow the payment instructions shown during checkout and keep any transaction reference required to verify the payment.

Order Status

An order's status may depend on successful payment verification and order processing. If a payment is deducted but the order is not confirmed, contact customer support with the order and transaction details. Never share your bKash PIN or OTP.

4. Privacy Policy

Nishat Fashion may process information that customers provide while using the website, creating an account, placing orders or contacting customer support.

Information Used

  • Account information, such as name and email address.
  • Contact and delivery information supplied by customers.
  • Order, cart and wishlist information needed for website features.
  • Messages submitted to customer support.

How Information Is Used

Information may be used to operate customer accounts, process orders, coordinate deliveries, provide support, maintain website security and improve the shopping experience.

Service Providers and Security

Website services may process information to provide functionality such as authentication, database operations, payment processing and image hosting. Access should be limited to what is necessary for those services.

Data Requests

Customers may contact support with questions about their information or requests concerning account data. Such requests are handled subject to applicable law, legitimate business requirements and technical limits.

5. Terms & Conditions

By using the Nishat Fashion website, visitors agree to use the service lawfully and provide accurate information when placing orders.

Product Information

Product descriptions, images, prices, availability and stock information are provided to help customers make purchasing decisions. Display differences may occur due to device settings or product updates.

Orders and Availability

Orders are subject to the availability and details confirmed by the store. If an order needs clarification or cannot be fulfilled, customer support should communicate the available next steps.

Website Use

Visitors must not misuse the website, attempt unauthorized access, interfere with its operation or submit deliberately misleading information.

Policy Changes

These policies may be updated when store operations or applicable requirements change. The published version should clearly reflect the store's actual practices and any required notices.
