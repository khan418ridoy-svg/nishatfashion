When you create an account, place an order or contact support, the site may process details such as your name, phone number, delivery address, email address and order information, depending on the fields you submit.

How Information Is Used

  • To process orders, arrange delivery and provide customer support.
  • To maintain order history and help prevent misuse or unauthorized activity.
  • To operate, troubleshoot and improve the website.

Website Services

The website may use third-party services for functions such as authentication, database storage, image hosting and payment processing. Those providers may process information as needed to provide their services, subject to their own terms and privacy practices.

Data Security and Retention

Reasonable technical and organizational measures should be used to protect customer information. Information may be retained for as long as needed for order records, customer support, security or legal obligations.

Your Questions and Requests

If you have a question about information associated with your account or order, contact Nishat Fashion through the Contact Us page. Requests may be subject to identity verification and applicable requirements.

Policy Updates

This page may be updated when website features or business practices change. The published policy should accurately describe the services and data practices actually in use.
